Shake & Ask · Support

Think of a yes-or-no question, then shake your phone or tap the ball. You can also make your own balls with your own answers.

Magic Answer Ball is for entertainment only. Its answers are random and are not real predictions or advice.

Common questions

Shaking does nothing. Shake the phone briskly, or simply tap the ball.

No sound. Check that Sound is on in the app's Settings and that your phone is not in silent mode.

No vibration. Check Vibration in the app's Settings and your phone's touch feedback setting.

Where are my balls saved? Only on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
